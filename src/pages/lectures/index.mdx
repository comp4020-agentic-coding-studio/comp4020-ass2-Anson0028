Each lecture makes one claim and shows a number from the specimen that backs
it. Five readings run
through them, one every two or three weeks. The label on each row says what the
week puts on trial: you, the game, the measuring instrument, or, in week 12,
your verdict. Weeks 6 to 8 are the block where the game is in the dock. Every week has
its slides, and the page under them is where the numbers live.
