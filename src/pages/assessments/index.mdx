Four pieces, and each one is built out of the one before it. Assignment 1
produces a curve. Assignment 2 picks one point on that curve and measures what
else moved. The lab book is the record of how you got there. The final is that
one number, defended.

Nothing here is marked on whether your change made the game better. All of it
is marked on whether someone else could check.
