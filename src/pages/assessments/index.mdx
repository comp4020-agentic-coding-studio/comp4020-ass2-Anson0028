Four pieces. Assignment 1 produces a curve. Assignment 2 picks one point on
that curve and measures what else moved. The final is that one number,
defended. The tutorial quizzes run alongside, one number a week, and are the
record of how you got there.

Nothing here is marked on whether your change made the game better. All of it
is marked on whether someone else could check.
