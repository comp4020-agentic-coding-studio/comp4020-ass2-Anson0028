## Before the studio

Nothing. Do not play it early. The first ten minutes only happen once.

## In the studio

Play for ten minutes with a notebook open. Write as you go and do not tidy it
up. "I didn't know what the bar was." "I keep walking past the orbs." "I stood
still and nothing died." That register.

Then go down the list with a neighbour and mark each line: could my
simulation have produced this? Most of the interesting ones are a no.

Pick one no. Make your instrument able to see it. A player that stands still,
a player that misses pickups by a body width, a metric for time spent not
moving.

## Afterwards

The raw list goes in the lab book unedited. The new check joins your
instrument for the defence.
