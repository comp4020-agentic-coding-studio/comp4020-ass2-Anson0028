## Before the tutorial

Have the whole defence ready. This is a rehearsal, and rehearsing half a thing
teaches you nothing about the other half.

## In the tutorial

Groups of four. One presents for ten minutes; three listen with the list of
seven parts in front of them and tick them off. The first missing tick is the
first question.

The most common gap is part six. People show that their change worked and
forget to show that their instrument could have told them if it had not.

## Where it goes wrong

- **Before and after on different seeds.** Then the difference is partly the
  seeds. Same 51, both times, and say so out loud.
- **One player.** A change that helps the player you tested can do nothing for
  the one you did not. Show at least two.
- **A change the size of the noise.** If your median moved 6 s, and week 9 told
  you a second set of seeds moves it 8.9 s, you do not yet have a change.
- **Not rerunning the lazy players.** Part five is the one people skip when they
  are short of time, and it is the one the panel asks about.

## What a finished answer looks like

The course's own worked change, for the shape and not for copying:

> Starting hearts, 3 to 4. The 100 ms player: 12 of 51 to 23 of 51, median
> 101.6 s to 117.2 s, same seeds. On a second set of 51: 7 to 20, so it holds.
> The 50 ms player: 38 to 43. The 150 ms player: 2 to 3, so it does almost
> nothing for the people who needed it most. Running away: 1 win to 2. Standing
> still: 17.4 s to 20.6 s. Still to show: that no card went dead.

## Afterwards

You have until the final submission to close whatever gap your group found.
