## Before the tutorial

Have the whole defence ready. This is a rehearsal, and rehearsing half a thing
teaches you nothing about the other half.

## In the tutorial

Groups of four. One presents for ten minutes; three listen with the list of
seven parts in front of them and tick them off. The first missing tick is the
first question.

The most common gap is part six. People show that their change worked and
forget to show that their instrument could have told them if it had not.

## Afterwards

You have until the final submission to close whatever gap your group found.
