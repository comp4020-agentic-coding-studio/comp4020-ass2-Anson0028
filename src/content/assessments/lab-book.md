## The brief

> Write it down on the day, with the number in it.

Studios in the first five weeks are unmarked so that you can be bad at this in
private. From week 6 the lab book counts.

It is marked on completeness and not on results, for a reason. A lab book that
only records the measurements that went well is an advertisement. The entry
where your metric turned out to be saturated is worth exactly as much as the
one where everything worked.

## What you submit

One file or one notebook, six dated entries. Photographs of handwriting are
fine if the numbers are legible.

## What it feeds

At your defence, any number you quote can be checked against the entry it came
from.
