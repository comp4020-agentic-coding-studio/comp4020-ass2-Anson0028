## The brief

> Change one number in Close Quarters. Convince three people who have spent a
> semester learning to distrust numbers.

The largest share of the mark goes to part six: where your instrument could be
lying. Showing that a change worked is the easy half. Showing that your
measurement would have told you if it had not worked is the half this course
exists to teach.

A defence of "I changed nothing, and here is why the current value is right"
is allowed and is harder than it sounds.

## What you submit

A recording of no more than ten minutes, your fork, and your twelve quiz sheets. The
panel is three classmates and one member of the teaching team, and they ask
questions from the recording.

## How the four pieces fit

Assignment 1 produces a curve. Assignment 2 picks a point on it and checks the
blast radius. The quizzes record everything in between. The defence is the
point, the radius and the record, said out loud.
