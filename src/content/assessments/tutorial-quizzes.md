## The brief

> Write the number down on the day, before you have decided what it means.

Every tutorial ends with the same three questions on one sheet: what did you
measure today, what was the number with its unit and its run count, and what
did you expect it to be before you started. Five minutes, then you leave.

They are marked on completeness and not on results, for a reason. A record
that only holds the measurements that went well is an advertisement. The week
your metric turned out to be saturated is worth exactly as much as the week
everything worked.

## What you submit

Nothing outside the room. The sheet is handed in at the end of each tutorial.
The two lowest of the twelve are dropped, so two missed weeks cost nothing.

## What it feeds

At your defence, any number you quote can be checked against the quiz it was
first written on.
