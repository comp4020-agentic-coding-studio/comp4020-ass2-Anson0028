Tamsin started simulating players in order to end an argument sooner. It
worked, the argument moved to whether the simulated player was any good, and
that turned out to be a better argument. This course is the long version of it.

She gives all twelve Tuesday lectures and marks the final defences.

## Reaching her

- **Consultation:** Tuesdays, 14:00 to 15:00, straight after the lecture. No
  booking. Bring the number you are stuck on and the run count behind it.
- **Email** for extensions, and ask before the deadline rather than after it.
- She does not answer "is my change good?" by email. Nobody can, without the
  table.

## Who to ask first

The person next to you in the tutorial, then Wei, then Tamsin. Most questions in
this course are "why did my number move?", and the fastest answer comes from
someone who can look at your screen.
