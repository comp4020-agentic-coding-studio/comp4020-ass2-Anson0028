# Every boss is easy for somebody

SLOP2875 · Week 11

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## “Is the first boss too hard?”

has no answer.

“For whom?” does.

---

## The claim

A game does not have a difficulty.

It has a different difficulty for every player who picks it up.

A balance decision is a decision about which of those players you are willing to lose.

---

## The same game, six players

Only the reaction time changes. 51 runs each.

| Reaction time | Past boss 1 | Past boss 2 | Win |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 50 ms | 50 | 39 | 38 |
| 100 ms | 47 | 22 | 12 |
| 150 ms | 38 | 5 | 2 |
| 200 ms | 18 | 2 | 0 |
| 250 ms | 11 | 0 | 0 |
| 300 ms | 10 | 1 | 0 |

---

## Read across a row

and you get one player's way through the game.

## Read down a column

and you get what a gate does to a crowd.

---

## The first boss

lets through 50 of the fastest players.

And 10 of the slowest.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# For the 200 ms player, two thirds of runs end at the first boss

---

## Whether that is right

depends on who the game is for.

It is a number now, so it can be argued about.
