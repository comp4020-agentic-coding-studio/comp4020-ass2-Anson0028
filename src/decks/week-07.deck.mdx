# A gate everyone passes asks no question

SLOP2875 · Week 7

---

## Twice a run, a boss with a clock on it

Kill it inside 26 s or the run ends.

The boss exists to ask one question: did you pick up enough on the way here?

---

## The claim

A gate only asks its question if a player who did nothing fails it.

Whether they fail is arithmetic.

You can do the arithmetic before anyone fights the boss.

---

## The multiplication

The opening weapon: 17 damage every 0.85 s.

That is 20 damage a second.

Over a 26 s window, 520 hp.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Any boss under 520 hp is no gate

---

## The first boss shipped with 200 hp

Players who declined every card walked through it.

11 runs out of 15.

It looked like a gate and asked nothing.

---

## What it asks now

| Upgrades held on arrival | Boss health |
| --- | --- |
| one or two | 884 hp |
| three | 680 hp |
| four | 510 hp |
| six | 340 hp |

The starting weapon counts as one. The line crosses 520 hp at four.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## The gate's question, in plain words

“Have you picked up at least three cards in the first 40 s?”

---

## Measured over 51 runs

Ordinary players arrive holding a median of 7 cards. 47 are alive after the window closes.

Players who decline every card: 0 out of 51.

---

## Upgrades banked, not level reached

A player who refuses every card still levels up.

The first version handed that player a weaker boss for it.
