# A complaint about balance is a prediction

SLOP2875 · Week 1

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# “The boss is too hard.”

---

## That is not an opinion

It is a prediction.

Nobody has written it down yet.

---

## Every balance argument hides a number

“Too hard” means fewer players get past this point than the designer meant.

How many? Out of how many?

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# An argument nobody can lose is not worth having

---

## One game, twelve weeks

**Close Quarters.** A run lasts 120 s.

The first boss arrives at 40 s. Kill it within 26 s or the run ends.

You will not play it until week ten.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## An opinion

“You can skip the upgrades and still get past the first boss.”

---

## The same thing, as a prediction

A player who takes no upgrades is still alive after 66 s

in at least 1 run out of 15.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# 0 out of 15

---

## What it cost to find out

15 runs

63 ms

Less time than it took to read this slide.

## Thursday

Three predictions about Close Quarters.

Each with a number, a unit and a run count.

No playing.
