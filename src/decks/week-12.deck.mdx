# Defending a number

SLOP2875 · Week 12

---

## Eleven weeks learning to distrust a number

This week you commit to one.

---

## The claim

A balance change is finished when someone else can check it.

“It feels better now” is where this course started.

It is not allowed at the end.

---

## A defence, in seven parts

One constant. Its old value and its new one.

Then seven things, in order, a minute or less each.

---

## 1 · The prediction

Made before measuring. With its number and unit.

## 2 · The instrument

Which simulated players. What reaction times. 51 runs on fixed seeds.

---

## 3 · The result

A distribution, not a single figure.

Wins out of 51 and median survival, before and after.

## 4 · What you took away

Restricted play: the change did not quietly kill an option.

---

## 5 · Who tried to break it

The lazy strategies from week eight, run again against the new value.

## 6 · Where the instrument could be lying

The ceiling, floor or cap nearest your metric, and why you are clear of it.

---

## 7 · What you felt

when you played the changed game.

And whether it agrees.
