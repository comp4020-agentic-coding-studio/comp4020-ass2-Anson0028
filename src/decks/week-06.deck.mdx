# A dead option looks like a live one

SLOP2875 · Week 6

---

## Three cards every level

An earlier build of the specimen had seven cards in the pool.

Three of them did nothing.

---

## The claim

An option that does nothing is indistinguishable, to the player choosing it, from one that matters.

Nothing happens when you pick it.

Nothing happening is not an event.

---

## How they were found

Damage, attack rate and pickup reach each measured within 1.5 s of taking no upgrade at all.

25 runs.

---

## Why they were dead

Damage was dealt per second of contact.

A weapon that spun faster touched each enemy more often, for proportionally less time.

The attack-rate card was worth exactly nothing.

---

## Two ways to ask

**Take only this.** Several levels of one card, refuse everything else.

**Never take this.** Choose normally, but one card is banned. Count what is lost.

They disagree.

---

## Take only this

Movement speed, the 150 ms player, median survival as speed levels are added:

65.3 s, 66.0 s, 52.4 s, 45.5 s.

Speed looks harmful. A fast player with slow reactions runs into things.
