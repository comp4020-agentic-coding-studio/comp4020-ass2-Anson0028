# A perfect player measures a game nobody will play

---

## The first simulated player

Re-decided its direction every frame. Every 16 ms.

Fifteen runs, ordinary upgrade choices.

It won 12.

---

## On those numbers

The game was too easy.

It wanted harder bosses.

---

## Then the player was made to wait

Re-decides about every 150 ms, not every 16.

Holds its last direction in between.

Aims up to nine degrees off.

---

## Same fifteen seeds

| Reaction time | Wins out of 15 | Median survival |
| --- | --- | --- |
| none | 12 | 120.0 s |
| 100 ms | 3 | 103.4 s |
| 150 ms | 1 | 81.5 s |
| 250 ms | 0 | 60.7 s |

---
