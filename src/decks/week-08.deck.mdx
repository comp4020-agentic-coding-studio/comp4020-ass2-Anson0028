# The lazy strategy must lose

SLOP2875 · Week 8

Swipe, or press →

---

## One idea under Close Quarters

Getting stronger means walking back into the fight.

For under an hour one evening, that was false. Nothing on screen showed it.

---

## The claim

A strategy that wins regardless of what the game throws at it turns every other option into decoration.

Players find it eventually.

A simulation finds it in a second, if you think to write the lazy player as well as the good one.

---

## The hour running away was correct

Experience was scattered generously across the arena.

A player that only ever fled: median 102 s.

A player that went looking for experience: 87 s.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Free experience had reversed the premise

---

## Two changes put it back

Experience dropped by a kill became worth 2.5 times the experience lying free.

Nothing is collected at a distance any more. You walk onto it.

---

## Where it stands

Three strategies, the 100 ms player, 51 runs each.

| Strategy | Median survival | Wins out of 51 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| go for the experience | 101.6 s | 12 |
| run away | 52.5 s | 1 |
| stand still | 17.4 s | 0 |
