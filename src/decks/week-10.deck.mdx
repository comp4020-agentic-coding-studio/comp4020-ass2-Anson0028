## Nine weeks of measuring

## The claim

Simulation answers the questions you thought to ask.

Playing asks the ones you did not.

Neither is the backup for the other.

---

## Standing still killed nothing

The opening weapon was a shard circling the player.

Stand still for six seconds: no kills. The shard was a point, and hit one enemy in five.

Every simulated run was full of kills, because a simulated player is always moving.

---

## 31 kills, level three

A person playing for 45 s killed 31 enemies and was still on level three.

43 pieces of experience on the field. The player walked past nearly all of it.

A simulated player steers onto a three-pixel dot without effort. A person does not.

---

## Nobody knew what the bar was for

The run timer was a thin pale line along the top.

Someone who had played asked what it was.

No simulated player has ever been confused by an interface.
